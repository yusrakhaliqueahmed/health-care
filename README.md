# 🩺 SehatSaathi Pro

**Multilingual AI Healthcare Companion for Pakistan**

SehatSaathi Pro is an AI + doctor-hybrid healthcare platform built to bring reliable, easy-to-understand medical guidance to every home — especially rural and low-literacy households. Users can describe symptoms, check medicines, upload lab reports or X-rays, and find nearby doctors, hospitals, and pharmacies — all through voice or text, in their own language.

🔗 **Live App:** [sehatsaathi-pro.ai.studio](https://sehatsaathi-pro.ai.studio)

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **AI + Doctor Hybrid Model** — AI drafts guidance instantly; a licensed doctor reviews and approves every case before it's treated as final.
- **Symptom Checker** — Voice or text intake with structured, conversational follow-up questions and clear severity guidance (Green / Yellow / Red).
- **Medicine Check** — Verify a medicine by name, photo, or symptom description, with age-aware dosage and safety guidance.
- **Report & X-ray Analysis** — Upload a photo, gallery image, or short video of a lab report or X-ray for a plain-language explanation.
- **Nearby Care Finder** — Locate doctors, hospitals, and pharmacies by GPS, manual entry, or voice.
- **Emergency Support** — Real-time red-flag detection with instant hospital/ambulance direction.
- **My Records** — Every symptom check, medicine check, and report is saved to the user's account and persists across logins and devices. Records can be viewed, copied as text, or downloaded as a formatted PDF.
- **Voice + Text Everywhere** — Every AI response is always available as both spoken audio and written text, with pause/resume/stop/replay controls.
- **Multilingual Support** — Fully working in **English**, **Urdu**, and **Roman Urdu**, in both voice and text, across every screen.
- **Google Sign-In** — Quick, secure login with the user's name and avatar shown in the sidebar.
- **Fully Responsive** — Consistent, polished experience across mobile, tablet, and desktop.

---

## ⚠️ Medical Disclaimer

SehatSaathi Pro is an AI-assisted guidance tool and **does not replace a licensed doctor**. All AI-generated suggestions are reviewed and confirmed by a registered doctor before being treated as final. Users are always encouraged to consult a licensed medical professional before taking any medicine or starting treatment, and to seek immediate emergency care when advised.
